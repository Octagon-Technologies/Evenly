Evenly — claim without the app v2

Mobile web. A guest scans the QR on that specific expense, claims what they ate, and leaves as a placeholder in the group that outlives the bill. Built on design.css tokens. Each row scrolls sideways.

Duplicate names are blocked, not warned · existing placeholders show evidence rows to jog memory · payer approves edits one at a time · per-expense QR/share link replaces nudging · claimed lines are never hidden · the claim list is chips only, no prose · the install prompt is one line at the foot of the page, never a card and never a button.
