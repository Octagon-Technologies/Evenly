# Evenly trip commercial: working creative brief

## Format and story

- 30-second, vertical social ad.
- Five friends arrive in Puerto Rico. The opening shows the destination and group; subsequent shots focus on the action in Evenly.
- One Airbnb stay becomes a group expense split evenly five ways. An Uber ride becomes another expense. At dinner, a photographed receipt becomes itemized dishes, everyone picks what they had, and Evenly shows each person's share including applicable tax, tip, or gratuity. The group sees clear balances and can settle through their own payment apps. Close on free trip tracking.

## Selected visual direction

- Opening: keep the warm Puerto Rico arrival scene. Latest copy revision: [`stills/copy-revision/arrival-question.png`](stills/copy-revision/arrival-question.png).
- After arrival: clean, light background with large, legible dark-mode Evenly UI. Give the app and the action most of the frame.
- Stay: [`stills/copy-revision/stay-airbnb-evenly.png`](stills/copy-revision/stay-airbnb-evenly.png) is the latest composition. The booking and matching app expense sit beside each other without a graphic arrow or connector.
- Dinner: use the arrow-free [`stills/copy-revision/dinner-scan-pick.png`](stills/copy-revision/dinner-scan-pick.png). The physical receipt, individual item cards, and matching phone rows carry the transition.
- Do not use visible arrows or connector graphics in the ad unless the owner later changes this choice.

## Current on-screen copy

- Arrival: “Going on a trip with friends?”
- Stay: “Split your AirBnb evenly”
- Dinner: “At restaurants, scan the receipt and let everyone pick what they ate”
- Keep these captions smaller than the earlier concept headlines so the action is primary.
