The join pill, three ways.

Same shape, same copy, same white button hard right, no green dot. The only variable is the black artwork behind the blue. Each is shown on the page surface it will sit on, then on dark, then at mobile full width where the gap between copy and button is widest.

A  ·  Split

The app's own split glyph, blown up far past icon size and cut by the right edge, with the arrowheads marching into the gap. It is the one mark that already means something specific in this product, and at this scale it stops reading as an icon and starts reading as texture.

B  ·  Receipt

Two receipts sliding in from the right with their torn edges showing and the ledger rules cut back out in the base colour. The most literal of the three, and the one that ties the pill to the thing the page spends its whole length arguing about.

C  ·  Ripple

Rings originating at the pill's midpoint, right where the copy ends, thick at the source and thinning as they grow off the right end. It carries no product meaning at all, which is the argument for it: pure surface, so it never competes with the two words beside it and cannot date the way a drawn receipt can.
