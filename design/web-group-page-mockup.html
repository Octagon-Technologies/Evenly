Strip width and colour, side by side

You asked for 2dp. On a 14dp-radius card, 2dp reads clearly in light but gets faint in dark, so 3dp is shown for comparison. Brand blue is the pick because owed is green, credit is amber, and errors are red. Blue says nothing about money.

What to look at
  • The strip never explains itself. No legend or tooltip. Alice's rows have it in All; in Mine every row has it, which is how the meaning sinks in.
  • Settled expenses keep their strip (the airport Uber): it means "you were on this", not "you still owe".
  • One thing that might clash: the unread dot on a row is also blue. A row can have both (Groceries). I think they read as different things (an edge vs. a dot), but say if it bothers you.
  • "Not you?" under the group name lets a guest switch names; that also covers a shared phone.
  • The web page uses the same link as the invite (/j/). If the app is installed, the link opens the app instead.
