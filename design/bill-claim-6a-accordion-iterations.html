6A — accordion behavior

Three ways to preserve multi-select while making completed and unfinished items unmistakable.

Separate iteration · Sep 2026
AStable receipt order

Nothing moves. Opening any card collapses the previous one. Assigned items use a blue check; unfinished items keep their receipt number.

Recommended: predictable, no invented completion step.

BAdvance when collapsed

Receipt order stays fixed. Selecting people does nothing to the card. When the user closes it, the earliest unfinished item opens automatically.

Faster, but the app chooses the next item.
