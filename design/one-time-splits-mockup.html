Quick splits, and payments either side can fix

Two related changes. Part 1 splits a one-off bill with no group: tap +, Split a bill, add the bill, type names, share the link. Under the hood it is a hidden group (groups.kind = 'quick'), so split-by-items, split-evenly, the web link and settle-up are all reused. Part 2 applies everywhere, groups included: the two people in a payment (the one paying back and the one owed) can both change it at any time. The change takes effect immediately, notifies the other person, and is kept in history. Nobody else can touch it.

Part 2 · Recording and fixing a payment (groups and quick splits)

The example: Bob paid the bill. Alice and Steve owe Bob. In the app, "Alice paid Bob" can be changed by Alice or Bob, and only them. Steve sees it but can't open it to edit. Every change applies immediately, sends the other person a notification, and adds a line to history.

7Confirm step, with the amount editableThis is the existing "We opened Venmo. Did the payment go through?" sheet, with one change: the amount is pre-filled and editable, so a partial payment ($15 of $21.05) is recorded honestly in one step. Once saved, it counts. It's not pending on anyone.

Decisions captured here

DecisionChoice
Data modelHidden group: groups.kind in ('group','quick'). Reuses members, typed-in names, web claim, settlements and merge unchanged. "Make it a group" just flips kind.
HomeOne list, no tabs. A quick split moves to History a day after it's fully settled.
PeopleNames only in v1. v2: system contact picker (no permission) fills in the name and keeps the number to text the link. v3 (later): verified phone numbers so app users get a push instead.
Billing / ProOut of scope for launch. Quick splits ignore group_passes entirely; revisit as a separate issue.
Who edits a paymentOnly the two people in it (the one paying back and the one owed). Not the admin, not other members. Enforced on the server, not just hidden in the UI.
When it appliesImmediately. No pending state. The other person gets a push, and history keeps before/after/who.
Settle-up payment editEdit the total only; the per-bill list is a read-only preview. Reducing cuts from the newest bill first, capped at what each bill had (nothing moves to other bills). Increasing fills the oldest unpaid bill first. Edited from one bill's screen, it changes only that bill.
Confirm stepExisting "Did the payment go through?" sheet gains an editable amount. Saved = counts.
Web guestsCan correct their own payment from the same link and browser.
