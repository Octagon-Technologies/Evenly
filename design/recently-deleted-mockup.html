Delete a group, and get it back

Deleting removes the group for everyone, not just you. It lands in Recently deleted for 30 days, where anyone who was a member can restore it. After 30 days it is permanently erased, including from our servers. Every member is told who deleted it, so a bad delete is visible and one tap from being undone.
