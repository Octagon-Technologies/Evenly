Mobile

The top of the page, unpacked

Your note about the hero feeling condensed was a spacing problem: title, field and button were stacked on a single small gap. Now the gaps are graded — 16px title→subhead, 26px subhead→field, 10px field→button — so the form reads as one object with air around it rather than three things jammed together. Field and button are full width and 52px tall, which is also a comfortable thumb target.
