Rules

• Create with an exact group name ("Jesse") ticks the existing Jesse instead of making a duplicate.
• Deselecting everyone and tapping Done does nothing silently: the footer reads "Pick at least one person" and Done shakes.
• One-time split (no group): no group section, only the create row and the people already on the bill.
• The chip on the card reads "Add person".

2. Scan before picking people: keep the jump, add a checkpoint

I wouldn't block the scan until people are picked. Scanning first is the fast path, and the bill already defaults to the whole group. The real problem is that "Review the receipt" hides who's in, and its only help is "Go back to add people". So the review screen gets a people row and a paid-by row at the top, both editable in place. You never have to go back.
