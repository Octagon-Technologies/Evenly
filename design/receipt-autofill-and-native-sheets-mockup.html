Receipt autofill, auto-category, native sheets

Three changes, mocked before any Compose. Decisions already made: read automatically on attach; no date field; iOS close ✕ top-left; Android follows Material 3's default (drag handle, no ✕). Filled fields carry no marker; they look exactly as if you had typed them.

1 · "Split one amount": attach a receipt, the form fills itself

The quick read runs on Haiku in one pass: total, cleaned merchant name, kind of expense, category. It only fills fields that are still empty and never overwrites what you typed. Filled fields look like any other field: no badge, outline or banner, since you can change any of them anytime. Once a receipt is attached, "Add" becomes "Replace" (one receipt per expense on this path). Whatever happens, the receipt stays attached.

2 · Category picks itself from the title

No AI call while typing. In order: this group's earlier expenses with a similar title, then the receipt read, then a fixed keyword list. The category is simply selected, with no badge or "suggested" label; you can change it like any pick. Once you choose one yourself, the guess never touches it again.

3 · Sheets that look native on each platform

One shared sheet component switches its top bar per platform, so all 26 places that use a sheet change together. Both platforms gain swipe-down to close (only the draggable sheet had it) and Android gains the back gesture, which currently doesn't close sheets at all.

  • iOS: a grabber; a floating rounded card at half height, joining the screen edges when full height (as iOS 26 sheets do); a round glass close button top-left (44pt circle, xmark with rounded stroke ends) with the title centered on the same line. Sheets with a confirm action put a ✓ top-right in the same style.
  • Android: Material 3 bottom sheet, a 32×4 drag handle, left-aligned title, dimmed backdrop, no ✕. It closes with a swipe down, a tap outside, or the back gesture with the predictive-back shrink. TalkBack reads the handle as "Drag handle, double-tap to close", so screen-reader users still have a close.
  • Centered dialogs (the modal card) keep a ✕ on iOS; on Android they become a standard Material dialog with a text "Cancel" button.
