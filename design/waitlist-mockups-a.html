Direction A — three cuts, less to read

Same bet as before: the scan demo does the explaining. The change is that the copy stops repeating it. Each version below has a hard word budget for everything above the fold, and none of them describe the receipt scan in a sentence — you watch it happen instead. The three-step "how it works" block is gone in all three; it was restating the animation in prose.

A1

Six words, then the proof

24 words above the fold

The tightest possible version of the original. Headline is four words; one line underneath carries the whole differentiator. Everything else that used to be prose is now a chip — "No app for friends", "Free", "No bank details" — which people scan rather than read. Safest pick: familiar layout, nothing to decode, demo sits at natural eye level.

Copy audit

What got cut, and why it was safe to cut it

"Take one photo and Evenly reads the line items off it" → deleted; the beam does it. The three-step how-it-works block → deleted in all three; it narrated the animation. "no account, no what-was-your-Venmo-again" → deleted; jokes cost a line and earn nothing on a page nobody is reading. Four-stat proof strip → three chips, two words each. The one line that survived everywhere is the differentiator: only you need the app. If a sentence isn't that, it isn't earning its place above the fold.
