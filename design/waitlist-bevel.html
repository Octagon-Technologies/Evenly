Built for privacy

We never see a bank detail, because we never ask for one.

Evenly works out who owes whom. Paying each other is something you already know how to do, in an app you already trust.

No bank details, everNo card, no account number, no open banking connection. There is nothing to leak.
We do not move moneyYou settle in your own payment app. Evenly just stops counting.
Your data is not the productNever sold, never brokered. Export or delete it whenever you want.
