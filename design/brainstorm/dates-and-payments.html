Dates and payments

Two changes for now: bills carry the day they happened, read from the receipt when it's there, and payments get recorded and edited on the bill itself, by either person. Adding several receipts at once is sketched at the end as a future feature.

What changed in revision 4

  • Latest: each History line is the amount with one method chip, then the date and who recorded it underneath. "Nothing paid yet" is gone from the rows. The batch split is whatever you pick on the setup screen, with no automatic rule, since a table that orders many items can still want to split evenly.
  • The button is the balance. Each row ends in a tappable "210 left ›" chip. There's no word to learn.
  • History moved into the sheet. Tapping the chip opens the add-payment sheet, and that person's past payments sit in a collapsible "History" card between "Via / On" and "Record payment".
  • The bill screen is calmer. The "2 payments ⌄" lines under each row are gone, since the history now lives in the sheet.
  • Review bill uses two buttons (Flow 1): "Assign items" and "Share link". The one-button flow is removed.
Build now

A. The bill's date

The app already stores a date per bill and groups the list by day. Nobody can set it, though, and every new bill is dated in UTC, so a bill added just after midnight in Kenya lands on the day before. The scanner already reads the printed time for the "Dinner at Java House" name. It just needs to read the printed date too.

Two buttons, decided

  • Both paths are one tap from the review screen, and the choice is visible up front.
  • "Assign items" is the tonal button, "Share link" the filled one, since letting friends pick is less work for you.
  • An evenly split bill skips this step: there is nothing to assign.

Rules

  • The chip text: "Today", "Yesterday", or a short date ("Nov 20"). Add the year only when it isn't this year ("Dec 30, 2025").
  • Date only, never a time. The printed time stays inside the scanner and only feeds the bill's name.
  • Default is the phone's local today, not UTC. This is a real bug today regardless of the feature.
  • Distrust odd receipt dates. A future date, or one more than about 60 days back, is more likely a misread. Fall back to today and say so in one line.
  • 04/03/26 is ambiguous. Send the model today's date and the currency, and pick the reading that's closest to today without being in the future.

What changes in code

  • Scanner: add an optional printed_date field next to printed_time.
  • App: the date chip on the new-bill, review and edit screens, and a local-today helper in place of todayUtc().
  • Server and sync: nothing to do. The date already syncs, and the merge already treats it as its own field (date_updated_at).

Open, decided

  • Everyone sees every row, like a group chat. It matches how the app works today and needs no server change.
  • Built on what's there. The bill screen already lists who owes what. This adds the "left ›" chip to the rows you can act on, and the separate Payments section moves into each person's History.
  • Who gets the chip: the payer on every row, and each person on their own row only. Rows you can't act on show plain text, with no chevron.

The chip is the button

  • Why it works: what's left is what you came to change, so tapping it is "edit balance" with no word to choose. The "›" and the blue fill say it taps.
  • Paid in full becomes a green "✓ Paid ›" that opens the same sheet, so you can still edit or cancel a payment after it's settled.
  • History card: collapsed by default, showing "2 payments · 280". It's hidden entirely when there are no payments yet, as on Purity's sheet. Its background is the page grey, a step off the white sheet.
  • Long histories scroll inside the card, so "Record payment" never moves off screen.

Rules

  • Method is remembered. Pre-pick the last method you used, Zelle included. Save it on your account so it carries across devices, with the device as a fallback.
  • Currency is the bill's currency. Changing it is possible, but the button is muted on purpose. An amount in another currency goes through the same conversion Settle up already uses.
  • Paying twice is already caught. A payment can't go over what's left. The Balances tab already flags a "possible double payment" and offers to remove it. A payment made from a bill shows up in that check too.
  • The other person always hears about it, with "Edit" in their History. Cancelling a payment is a soft delete.

Your questions

  • Where do the receipts go? Each photo is attached to the bill it created, using the same upload as a single scan. It shows as the thumbnail on the card now, and in the bill's receipt viewer afterwards.
  • Fixing a wrong name or amount: the name has a pencil and can be edited on the card. Tapping anywhere else opens the sheet with the receipt above the fields. A wrong item total is fixed in the full review screen, the same one a single scan uses ("Edit items" on the sheet).
  • 5 people down to 3 from the same group: uncheck two people in "Who's in". The bill stays in the group. Picking "No group" from the group menu turns it into a one-time split with whoever you choose.
  • Three chips per card: group (grey), who's in (blue), split (green). A chip that differs from the defaults gets an outline, like the fuel receipt's "3 of 5" and "Evenly".

Decisions for you

How far back can a receipt date go?

Older dates are usually a misread. People can still set an older date by hand.

Suggest: 60 days, otherwise use today and say so
