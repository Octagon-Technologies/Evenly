3 · Recording a payment

By default the payment goes to her oldest debts first, as before. Tap Change and every amount becomes editable. Changing one never moves the others. A bar under the list shows how much is left to assign. Save always stays tappable: if the amounts don't match what she paid, tapping it says exactly what's off instead of greying the button out.
