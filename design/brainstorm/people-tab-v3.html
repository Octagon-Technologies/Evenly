3 · The open part

Which names belong in the pool?

Your worry is the right one. If Alex, Bob and Steve are in one group and Joe and James in another, those are almost certainly five different people. A pool that lists every lone name as "unlinked" turns your whole contact history into a to-do list. These rules keep the pool small and never make you feel you owe the app any linking:

A lone name is a person, not a loose endEvery name starts as its own person. Alex, Bob, Steve, Joe and James are five rows, with no badge, no count and no "unlinked" label. Linking only happens when you start it.
Joined accounts are never in the poolAnyone who joined a group or claimed their name with an account is already one person across all their groups. Only names you typed by hand can be linked.
Only names that owe youThe People tab only counts debts to you, so a name owing $0 has nothing to combine. Settled names stay out of the pool.
Never someone from the same groupOne person can't be in a group twice. When you're linking to Purity, anyone who shares a group with her is left out. Pookie can be Purity because Purity isn't in Boston. Maya can't be, because they're both in New York.
Four 10-person groups: 40 names→typed by hand: 26→owe you: 9→not in Purity's groups: 4

D · Link while adding. When you add someone to a new group, typing a name offers the people you already know. Picking one reuses that person, so they're linked from the start. Nobody ends up in the pool. It doesn't fix old groups, so it pairs with A, B or C.
My pick: D always, plus A for cleanup. D means new groups never create the problem. For old groups, A matches the moment you actually notice it: you see "Pookie $70", think "that's Purity", and link it in two taps. B is good as the ⋯ entry on her page if you want a second door.
Still open

Three smaller calls

Who sees a link?When you link Pookie to Purity, does anything change for the Boston group?
Suggestion: no. Links belong to your account and only change your People tab. Boston still shows "Pookie". They sync across your devices, so a new phone doesn't lose them.
Undoing a linkIf you got one wrong.
Suggestion: her page's ⋯ menu → "Names", where each name has "Unlink". The amounts move back to separate rows.
Carried over: "who you owe" and paying from her phonePurity still can't pay all her groups at once from her side.
Suggestion: show "You owe" only for people linked by their account, which is accurate on every phone.
