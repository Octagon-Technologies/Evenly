Brainstorm · People tab

Who owes you, across every group

One place that adds up what each person owes you from every group and one-time split. It shows the per-group breakdown, and you can record a payment with proof without opening each group. It only covers people who owe you. "Who you owe" waits until we can make it accurate.

Same data in every variation: Purity owes you $150 from Boston ($70, where you saved her as "Pookie"), New York ($45), New Jersey ($15) and one Uber split ($20). Debts never cancel against what you owe her. That number isn't shown here at all. Tap the cards in variation B to expand them.

A list on Home, a page per person

People becomes a third segment next to Groups and One-time on Home. Each row is one person and one green number. Tapping a person opens their page, which breaks the total down by group, oldest debt first. This is the closest match to how the app already works.

What I'd pick, and one gap to decide

Which layout?A is the safest to build and fits Home today. C answers "what is this for?" best. B is fastest to use but has nowhere to keep payment history.
My pick: A's list with C's timeline as her page. Group view and timeline as a toggle on her page.
Who records the payment?With only "who owes you" in v1, Purity won't see a People tab entry for you, so she can't pay all her groups at once from her phone. You record "Purity paid you" and attach the Zelle screenshot, so she still has to send it to you first. That's the WhatsApp step you wanted to cut.
My suggestion: also show "You owe" in v1, but only for people linked by their account (they joined or claimed the name). Those links come from the server, not one phone, so the totals are accurate. Typed-in names stay owed-to-you only until they're claimed.
Where does Pay land in each group?A $100 payment that covers three groups becomes three payments, one per group, all sharing one proof screenshot and marked as one batch. Undoing it removes all three.
My suggestion: yes, undo them together.
