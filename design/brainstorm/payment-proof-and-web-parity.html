Proof of payment, and a web page that shows everything

Someone says "I paid you $24 for the Uber." Today you have to take their word for it, or ask for a screenshot over text and then go record it yourself. This lets the person who paid attach the screenshot right where they record the payment, so the other person can check it in one tap. Section C brings the web bill up to the app: receipts, payment proof, payment history and comments.

The rule in one line

  • Attach where you record, see it where you check. The screenshot is added in the "You paid Andrew" sheet. It shows up in three places people already look: the bill's Receipt card, the person's row on the bill, and the History inside that same sheet.
  • Optional, never in the way. Recording a payment still takes one tap. Cash payments don't ask for anything.

Settle up (across bills) gets the same tile

  • The pairwise "Settle up" sheet on the group page gets the identical "Add a screenshot" tile. One Zelle transfer often covers several bills, so one screenshot attaches to the one payment, and each bill it covered shows it in its Receipt & payments card.
  • The group's payment list (Balances) shows 📎 on payments that have proof.

"Ask for one"

  • Sends Maya a push: "Andrew asked for a screenshot of your $10.00 Venmo payment." Tapping it opens her History with "Add proof" ready.
  • Only the person who received the money sees it, and once per payment, so it can't become nagging.
  • This is the girlfriend case exactly: she says she paid, you don't remember, you ask in one tap instead of a text thread.

D. How it would be stored

New table, same shape as receipts

  • payment_proofs: id, settlement_id, group_id, uploaded_by, storage_path, mime_type, size_bytes, timestamps, soft delete. Not a nullable column on receipts, because older apps assume every receipt has an expense.
  • Keyed to the payment's chain, not the row. Editing a payment voids it and writes a replacement. Proof hangs off original_settlement_id ?? id, so a screenshot survives "change amount".
  • Uploads reuse the receipt outbox (resumable, survives the app closing). Server side first, per the sync rule.

Web and privacy

  • The web still ships no Supabase client: web-claim returns short-lived signed URLs for receipts and proof, and gets new actions for comments and proof upload.
  • Payment screenshots can show account numbers and balances. Proof is visible to the group only (same as receipts). Worth a one-line hint on the tile later if testers attach full banking screens.
  • Images only (PNG, JPEG, HEIC), one per payment, compressed on device like receipts.

Decisions for you

1. One screenshot per payment, or several?One covers Zelle/Venmo/M-Pesa. A bank transfer sometimes needs two pages.Recommend: one, replaceable. Easy to widen later.
2. Who can attach?The person who paid, or either of the two people in the payment (the receiver might screenshot their bank alert).Recommend: either of the two, same as who can edit a payment.
3. "Ask for one" in v1?It's the button that solves your Orlando case, but it's another notification type.Recommend: yes, once per payment.
4. Web orderViewing (receipts, proof, payment history, comments) is one release; adding proof and writing comments from the web is the second.Recommend: ship both together; adding proof is the point of the web half.
