Three tabs for what it is, one shelf for where it went

The five buckets you listed are really two different questions. All / Groups / One-time is what kind of thing it is. Archived / Recently deleted is where it is in its life. Mixing them into one tab bar is why it kept growing to five. Split the axes and both fit.

  • Tabs (max 3): All · Groups · One-time. Default All, and the choice is remembered per device.
  • Lifecycle lives at the bottom of whichever tab you're on, as one quiet "Put away" row. Its counts follow the tab, so on One-time it only counts one-time bills.
  • Tapping it opens your two-card accordion as a full screen, where the height rule you described works cleanly (the screen is the container).
  • History folds into Archived. Today Home already has three shelves: History (settled quick splits), Archived, Recently deleted. A settled one-time bill can just auto-archive after a day, which leaves exactly your two cards.
