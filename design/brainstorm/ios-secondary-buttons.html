iOS secondary button: plain, gray or outlined

Three real Evenly screens, one for each layout case, each drawn three ways. The main button is the same everywhere: a filled capsule with no shadow. Only the secondary button changes. Press and hold any button to see its pressed state.

1 · Stacked: "Discard your changes?"

DiscardChangesDialog is the classic "do it or back out" pair, stacked. About 16 of the 20 Secondary buttons look like this.

2 · Side by side: the Filter sheet

FilterSheet puts "Reset" and "Show 12 results" in one row of two equal halves. Group settings' "Share link" | "Copy link" has the same shape.

3 · Equal choices: Home welcome

HomeWelcome offers two real ways to start ("Split a one-time bill", "Start a trip group"), with a text link under them ("Join a friend's group"). Nothing here is a cancel.

Stacked: plain wins. It's what iOS's own setup screens and sheets do, and it puts the most distance between "do it" and "back out".

Side by side: plain looks lopsided. The left half of the row is just floating text, so the row reads as one button plus a stray label. Gray and outlined both fix that.

Equal choices: plain breaks the hierarchy. "Start a trip group" ends up looking exactly like the "Join a friend's group" link under it, so the three-step ladder collapses into a button and two links. Gray and outlined both keep three distinct levels.

Gray or outlined? In light mode, outlined is the crisper of the two and matches Android exactly. Gray is the more native iOS look, and it holds up better in dark mode, where a hairline on black gets faint. Either works. The real choice is per case: plain when stacked, and a shape when side by side or equal.
