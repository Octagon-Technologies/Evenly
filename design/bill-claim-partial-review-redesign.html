What changed and why

  • 3 buttons → 2, framed as a question. "Finish later" and "Finished picking mine" only differ by the viewer's own done marker. "Done here? Your picks are saved." frames both buttons as exits; the buttons state whether your own picks are complete, mapping 1:1 to no-op / markDone.
  • "Continue assigning" becomes per-item Assign. The leftover card is the to-do list, and each row opens that item's editor directly. Better than jumping to "the first unresolved item".
  • Remaining work shown once. Progress bar, "2 left", "Not assigned yet $39", and the amber list collapse into the amber card plus a muted "Not assigned" row that makes the totals add to $86.00.
  • Your items listed under your name. The question is about your items, so the answer sits right above it.
  • Handoff promise sits beside the exits, as §2 requires, instead of in a banner at the top.

Spec edits this needs (§2 row 7, §4, AC 7, AC 12)

  • Replace the Continue assigning button with per-row Assign on the remaining-items card.
  • Rename exits: Finish later → I'll pick more later, Finished picking mine → I've picked all mine, under "Done here? Your picks are saved." Behavior is unchanged: only "I've picked all mine" calls markDone; both open expense details over group home.
  • "Every item with its assignee(s) in receipt order" stays reachable via See all 5 items; person rows open that person's items.
  • If a viewer has no items yet, the footer stays the same; "I've picked all mine" is still valid (they had nothing on this bill).
