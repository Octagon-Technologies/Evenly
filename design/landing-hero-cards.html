Motion sketch · rebuilt

Paired, with a door time of 0.9 s

Cards no longer arrive and leave independently. Each expense is now paired with its own result: the receipt flies in, sits hidden behind the phone for 0.9 s, and its answer leaves from the exact point the receipt went in at. Only one card is ever behind the device, so nothing accumulates in there. The readout bottom right shows in, hidden and out separately, and the hidden count never exceeds one.

Every card now crosses in 2.81 s, which is the restaurant receipt's old 3.375 s taken 1.2x faster. Receipts and chips run at the same speed, so nothing is singled out any more.

The acceleration is back and it is now a real number. Velocity ramps 1 to 1.25 on the way in, peaking as the card reaches the device, and 1.25 back to 1 on the way out, so a card bursts away from the phone and eases off toward the edge. The old power curve could not do this: it started at zero velocity, which is why the acceleration read as a crawl and then a lurch. This is a linear velocity ramp, integrated, so the card is always moving and the 1.25 is exact.

Evenly

Two copy edits pairing forced. Every output now has to be caused by the input that preceded it, which broke two sub lines. Maya owes you reads "Her round at Petley's" and is $18.00 rather than $38.60, because it now follows the bar tab and not the dinner. Items unclaimed reads "6 items unclaimed, Chandarana Market" rather than 5 at Tamarind Dhow. Diego keeps "Passion juice, service" because he genuinely follows the restaurant receipt. If you would rather keep the original copy, the alternative is letting some outputs be group level events with no paired input, which reopens the accumulation problem.
