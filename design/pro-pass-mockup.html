Frames 1-7 are the group pass, already specced and partly built. Frames 8-14 are the gap the owner reported: there is no way to see or buy Pro today, and the two gates that exist both dead-end. Frame 9 is the only screen here we do not draw in Compose, because it is built in the RevenueCat editor so it can be redesigned and A/B tested without an app release.

We do not write this screen. It is a RevenueCat paywall rendered by the Paywall() composable, so its layout, copy and price mix are a dashboard change and Experiments can test them without shipping a build. That is the entire reason the product grew a subscription (spec §2.1). Terms, Privacy and Restore are App Review requirements, not decoration.

"Manage or cancel" is not a euphemism and not an in-app imitation: it opens the store's own subscription screen. Cancelling has to be as easy as buying, and a subscriber who cannot find the cancel button writes the 1-star review the original spec was built to avoid. Listing the groups it covers is what makes a personal subscription legible in a group app, and it is the honest answer to "what am I actually paying for?"
